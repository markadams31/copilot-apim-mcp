# Icons

These icons are a subset of Microsoft's official icon sets, used under Microsoft's terms, which
permit their use in architectural diagrams, training materials and documentation:

- [Azure architecture icons](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/architecture/icons/) (V25)
- [Microsoft Power Platform icons](https://learn.microsoft.com/power-platform/guidance/icons)
- [Microsoft Entra architecture icons](https://learn.microsoft.com/entra/architecture/architecture-icons)

They're used unmodified (not cropped, recoloured or distorted), each next to its product name, as
the terms require. Microsoft reserves all other rights.

The repository's MIT licence does not apply to these files.
